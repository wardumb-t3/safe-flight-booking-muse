muse-vault

Let Muse book your flight without ever seeing your passport.

Get started → Scan your passport and connect your vault (about 3 minutes, once).

Then, in Muse: Create a custom connector for muse-vault, and paste the settings the setup page gives you.

  1. Scan your passport on your phone with the Private Doc Scanner. It's sealed to your own Terminal 3 vault.
  2. Sign in to Terminal 3 on the setup page, which connects the vault and gives Muse a limited agent key.
  3. Ask Muse for a flight. It searches, holds the seat, and pays with Link on a checkout page. Your passport goes from the enclave to the airline's booking system, never through Muse.
