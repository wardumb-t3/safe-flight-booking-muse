    Who sees your passport?
    
Your passport, date of birth, phone and email go from your Terminal 3 vault, inside a hardware enclave, straight to the airline's booking system (Duffel), and to the booking service operator as the seller. Muse and Meta never see them. Muse gets a key that can only search, hold a seat and check its status.
